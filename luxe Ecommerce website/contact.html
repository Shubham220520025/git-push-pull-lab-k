Frequently Asked Questions

What are your shipping options? +
We offer free standard shipping on orders over $100. Express shipping is available for $15, and overnight shipping for $25. International shipping is available to most countries.
What is your return policy? +
We offer a 30-day return policy for all unworn items with original tags. Items must be in original condition. Return shipping is free for exchanges within the US.
Do you offer size exchanges? +
Yes! We offer free size exchanges within 30 days of purchase. Simply contact our customer service team to initiate an exchange.
How can I track my order? +
Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can also track your order by logging into your account on our website.
